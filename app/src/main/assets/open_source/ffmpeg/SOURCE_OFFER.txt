BLOFY PLAYER FFmpeg source and relink materials

BLOFY PLAYER release builds that bundle FFmpeg use FFmpeg n6.0
(commit ea3d24bbe3c58b171e55fe2151fc7ffaca3ab3d2) under the GNU Lesser
General Public License version 2.1 or later.

The complete corresponding FFmpeg source, the Media3 1.6.1 decoder source used
to create libffmpegJNI.so, the build recipe, the FFmpeg and AndroidX Media
changes.diff files, and integrity hashes are distributed in:

  BLOFY-PLAYER-2.0-<release-label>-ffmpeg-compliance.zip

That compliance archive must be offered from the same download location and
for the same period as the matching APK or AAB. Do not redistribute the APK or
AAB by itself without the matching compliance archive.

The materials permit rebuilding libffmpegJNI.so with a modified,
interface-compatible FFmpeg library, replacing it in the matching APK, and
signing that modified APK with the recipient's own key for the recipient's own
use. BLOFY PLAYER does not restrict reverse engineering performed solely to
debug such LGPL modifications.

FFmpeg project: https://ffmpeg.org/
FFmpeg n6.0 source: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/tree/ea3d24bbe3c58b171e55fe2151fc7ffaca3ab3d2
AndroidX Media3 1.6.1 source: https://github.com/androidx/media/tree/839c4a90f2ab36e48be73e1b5e907f3283dce72e
