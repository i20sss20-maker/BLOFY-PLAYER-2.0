# BLOFY PLAYER — Owner-approved annual tiers (2026-11-09)

Scope: BLOFY PLAYER **55.8.7** development branch only. These are approved **commercial target prices**, not live Google Play products.

| Plan | Annual price (SAR) | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| FREE | 0 | User-supplied Xtream/M3U playback and essential features |
| PRO | 19 | Optional premium app experience and advanced features once implemented |
| VIP | 49 | PRO plus optional licensed content access, only if distribution rights are verified |

- Annual subscriptions only for PRO and VIP; no monthly or six-month product is approved.
- Do **not** include unlicensed IPTV or media under VIP; a free gift or undisclosed content still needs distribution rights.
- Do not present PRO/VIP as purchasable or enabled before Play Billing, backend purchase verification, lifecycle (renewal/cancellation/refund/restore), entitlement checks, compliant review access, and on-device QA are complete.
- Keep paid app features separate from BLOFY STREAM media entitlements and legacy device activations.
- Avoid automatic live-site publishing, rollout, or modifications to existing customers during development.
- For Play Console, use annual subscription products/base plans priced at SAR 19 and SAR 49 after confirming available pricing and applicable taxes/fees.
