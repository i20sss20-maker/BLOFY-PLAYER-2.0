# rc07.35 — Library recovery and mobile/account experience

## Receiver recovery

The reported receiver symptom is an import staying at 29% during Series, followed by the saved-list placeholder remaining after reopening. The available remote playback records do not contain that failed import, so they do not establish its exact root cause.

- Saved cards are read before identity reconciliation and QR rendering. An independent eight-second UI deadline replaces a stuck placeholder with Retry without deleting saved data. A cancelled identity refresh no longer holds Enter in an unbounded join.
- The Room database explicitly uses WAL on low-memory receivers so catalog writes do not monopolize the only connection needed by Login reads.
- Bulk streaming requests retain their Retrofit call until body parsing and Room batches finish. Leaving the screen or reaching the existing deadline cancels the underlying socket even after response headers arrived.
- First imports keep validated, source-bound section checkpoints. An interrupted Series download can reuse completed Live/Movies sections; partial sections never grant entry. Failed staged refreshes retain the prior committed library.

## Mobile and account tools

- Phone detail pages scroll around a persistent primary action and secondary actions. TV layouts keep their existing side-by-side structure.
- Phone playback has a touch toggle, seek bar, scrollable options and transient system bars. Rotation and closing player dialogs reapply fullscreen without recreating playback.
- Watchlist actions join the phone action row. Settings expose a lightweight interface preference and a metadata-only subscription check. These checks do not prove a channel can play and do not open a stream.
- The website adds a responsive landing page, download channels, a device account page and support requests. Admin has customer records, account checks, diagnostics, support resolution, release management and an audit trail. Existing device PIN and admin session authorization are retained.
- New database tables are additive. No customer, provider, activation or catalog rows are reset by this release.

## Verification

The local phone emulator passed actual movie/series portrait/landscape action visibility and real MP4 playback controls, rotation and fullscreen dialog recovery. Backend tests cover account metadata classification, private-address rejection, authentication, device ownership, release validation and existing service behavior. Local browser verification uses an isolated PostgreSQL-compatible database and fixture credentials, not customer data.

Additional cancellation, blocked-read/retry, WAL read/write and checkpoint regression tests accompany the change. The final build/CI and signed-artifact results are recorded with the release artifact. Physical receiver confirmation and the user's new hidden-host account result remain separate checks.

Media3 remains 1.6.1. Production signing identity, supported ABIs, TV live preview and playback transport policies are unchanged.
